Why people love Newton

Newton is the county seat of Catawba County, known for its historic courthouse square and small-town feel. It sits just south of Hickory, so you're minutes from shopping, jobs and everything the area offers.

  • Historic downtown and local events
  • More home for your money
  • Quiet neighborhoods close to Hickory
  • Easy commute in every direction
