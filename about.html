Meet Nolan Herman

Licensed REALTOR® with Nestlewood Realty, serving Hickory, Newton, Granite Falls and the Charlotte area.

My Story

I didn't wait for an invitation.

Real estate wasn't a family business, and I didn't have a traditional path into the industry. School wasn't for me, and after experiencing firsthand how stressful moving can be, I knew I wanted to build a career where I could make that experience easier for others.

I took a chance on myself, reached out for an opportunity, and turned that first yes into a career built on hard work, determination, and helping people find a place to call home.

Two years later, I'm a licensed REALTOR® with Nestlewood Realty. I've helped clients buy and sell over $10 million in homes, including more than $8 million in 2026 alone, and every one of my listings has sold. I bring that same hustle to every client: fast answers, honest advice, and marketing that gets results.
